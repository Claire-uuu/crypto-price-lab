The price is not one number.

When two exchanges report a price for the same cryptocurrency, what exactly are we comparing? An interactive explainer about market data, time, and the danger of oversimplifying a number.

02 / Why this matters

Numbers have definitions.

A last traded price, an hourly candle close, a bid, an ask, and a volume-weighted average are not interchangeable. Comparing observations without matching the product, timestamp, currency pair and measurement definition can produce a misleading story.

03 / Engineering connection

From pipelines to public understanding.

This independent project extends ideas encountered while building a multi-exchange cryptocurrency ingestion pipeline during a software engineering internship at VortexNet: normalization, timestamp alignment, deduplication and validation. It does not reuse employer-owned code or private datasets.

04 / Evidence standards

What would count as a finding?

A defensible comparison requires independently retrievable Coinbase and Kraken BTC/USD OHLC observations; the same UTC hourly windows; rejection of incomplete candles; explicit missingness; and an archived provenance record with collection time and content checksums.

Even a validated difference between hourly closing prices does not establish simultaneous executable quotes, an arbitrage opportunity, or a causal explanation for the discrepancy.

Official documentation: Coinbase candles · Kraken OHLC.

A software engineering portfolio, with an editorial question.
